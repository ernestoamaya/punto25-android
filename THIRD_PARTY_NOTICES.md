## Software and services

Current Android dependencies include, among others:

- Android / AndroidX libraries;
- Jetpack Compose and Material 3;
- Kotlin and Kotlin coroutines integrations;
- Google Maps Compose and Google Play Services Location;
- Firebase Authentication and Firebase BoM-managed libraries;
- Android Credentials and Google Identity libraries;
- JUnit for unit tests.
